What we build

Software for administrative and financial processes, designed to be used by non-technical people.

  • Web applications and multi-tenant SaaS

    Platforms where several organizations work with their own data, kept separate from one another.

  • APIs and backend systems

    The foundation behind applications, integrations and reports.

  • Integrationsbanks · payments · messaging

    Connections to payment gateways such as Flow and Khipu, bank statement uploads and messaging through the WhatsApp Cloud API.

  • Process automation

    Workflows that receive an event, such as a payment, and run tasks or notify the right person.

  • Accounting and management systems

    Client-server applications to record, reconcile and report financial information.
