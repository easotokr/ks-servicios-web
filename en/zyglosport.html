It all started in a club's treasury. When the role was taken on, nothing was handed over. When it was left, nobody asked for the records, even though they were in order.

That is the problem ZygloSport solves: members, payments and financial history stay in the club's system, and whoever takes over finds it all complete from day one.

What ZygloSport does for your club

Designed for treasurers and boards who run the club in their spare time.

  • Members and squads

    Records of members, players and categories, with their payment history and up-to-date status.

  • Fees and collections

    See who has paid, who owes and how much, without building spreadsheets every month.

  • Online paymentsFlow · Khipu

    Integration with payment gateways so members can pay from their phones and the payment is recorded automatically.

  • Bank reconciliation

    Upload your bank statement and the system matches each deposit to the right fee.

  • WhatsApp notifications

    Fee reminders and match notices sent to each member's WhatsApp, on a fixed plan based on club size.

  • One system, many clubs

    Each club works with its own data, separate from all others.

  • Treasury assistant in development

    An AI assistant to answer questions about the club's finances and support the treasurer.
